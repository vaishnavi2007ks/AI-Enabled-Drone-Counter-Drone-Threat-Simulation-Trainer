Comprehensive explanation of the platform's purpose, Explainable AI architecture, multi-sensor threat detection taxonomy, and academic implementation.

Project Purpose & Vision

The proliferation of commercial and ad-hoc unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) introduces significant security challenges for civil infrastructure, airports, and restricted facilities.

This project provides an interactive, safe, educational training environment for students, aerospace researchers, and security analysts to evaluate how AI algorithms detect and classify drone incursions without physical risks.

How AI Analyzes Aerial Threats

The simulated AI Threat Engine employs an ensemble multi-sensor fusion model inspired by real-world Counter-UAS (C-UAS) systems:

  • Kinematic Anomaly Detection: Evaluates trajectory derivatives, speed variations, and rapid descent profiles.
  • Geofence Spatial Indexing: Continuous point-in-polygon and Euclidean distance calculations relative to protected assets.
  • Digital Remote ID Verification: Validates FAA/ICAO cryptographic broadcast transponders against safe civil manifests.
  • RF Spectrum Signature Analysis: Detects frequency-hopping (FHSS) and high-bandwidth encrypted command downlinks.

Safe & Non-Kinetic Counter-Drone Framework

In compliance with civil aviation regulations and safety ethics, all recommended counter-drone actions are defensive and non-destructive:

1. Passive Optical Verification: Steer PTZ cameras to record high-resolution payload evidence.
2. Air Traffic Coordination: Broadcast automated safety NOTAMs to civil ATC towers.
3. Security Dispatch: Alert perimeter response officers with precise GPS coordinates.

Technical Stack & Deployment
